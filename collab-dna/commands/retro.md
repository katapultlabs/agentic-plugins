Audit the current conversation, not a past one. There is nothing to
extract; the transcript is in context.

1. Read this plugin's `skills/collab-dna/references/principles.md` and
   `references/audit-rubric.md`.
2. Apply the rubric to the conversation so far, from the first human
   turn to now. You were the agent: report honestly where your own
   output pulled the human off course, as the rubric's last section
   says, and keep the findings about the human's moves.
3. Produce the report in the rubric's shape. Quote the human. Rank by
   cost. End with the one instruction that should leave this
   transcript and go into CLAUDE.md or a memory, with proposed wording,
   and three lines for next session.

If the conversation is short (fewer than five human turns), say so and
give the two or three observations that apply rather than the full
table.

This is a good command to run before a compaction or at the end of a
long build. The "write this down" section is the part that survives.
